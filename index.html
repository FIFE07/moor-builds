Fair questions, honest answers

"I already have a Facebook page. Why do I need a website?"

Facebook is where people who already know you hang out. Google is where new customers search. A website makes you findable, lets you show your menu, prices and opening hours properly, and makes your business look established — because it is.

"I already have a website. Can you take it over?"

Yes — that's our redesign path, and it's the same plans at the same prices. One honest note: we don't patch old site-builders like Wix or Squarespace. We rebuild your site fresh on our faster, cleaner setup, move your content across, and only switch your domain over once you approve the new site. Your old site stays live the whole time — zero downtime.

"Can you just improve my SEO without a new website?"

Yes — that's our SEO Refresh (£90 one-off). We rewrite your page titles, descriptions, headings and image tags, submit your site to Google Search Console, and check your Google Business Profile. Honest promise: we improve everything Google reads — we never guarantee rankings, and neither should anyone else.

"Your prices seem low. What's the catch?"

No catch. We use a refined, repeatable process (the same one that built fife.earth), so what takes an agency weeks takes us days. You're paying for a professional result, not agency overheads.

"What if I don't like the design?"

You review a private preview before anything goes live, and every plan includes revision rounds — that's where you shape the result. The final 50% is due on completion, before the site goes live on your domain.

"What happens if Moor Builds disappears?"

You own everything: your domain, your hosting account, your website files. Nothing sits in an account only we control. If we vanished tomorrow, your website would keep running untouched.

"Will I be on page 1 of Google?"

No honest person can promise that — and you should run from anyone who does. What we do promise: correct titles, descriptions, structure and a Search Console submission, so Google can find and rank you properly. For most small local businesses, that's a genuine edge.

"I'm not technical. Will I cope?"

You never touch anything technical. You send us your details by email, we build, you review by tapping a link on your phone. Your handover pack explains everything in plain English.
